South Indian Chicken Biryani

Image sourced from licious.in

Description

Biryani is a staple dish at celebrations all across India. Weddings, graduations, births, deaths, you name it. My memories of this dish are those of my grandmother. No one will ever make it quite the way she did, but we can try.

Ingredients and Supplies (feeds 4)

  • 2 steel pots with lid (~7+ liters pot)
  • Large wooden spoon
  • Aluminum foil
  • 1 lb. of Basmati rice
  • 1 lb. of chicken thigh/leg meat, leave on the bone
  • 2 large yellow onions, sliced
  • 2 roma tomatoes, chopped
  • 1/2 cup of olive oil
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 tsp. ginger garlic paste
  • 1/4 teaspoon of pre-mixed garam masala
  • 1 heaping tsp. kashmiri chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp. turmeric
  • "Whole" Garam Masala (mix the following into a bowl and keep aside)
    • 4 cardamom
    • 4 cloves
    • 2 sticks of cinammon
    • 4-6 bay leaves
  • 1 bunch of cilantro
  • 4-6 fenugreek leaves
  • 4-6 mint leaves
  • Mix the previous three in the ratio 2:1/2:1, respectively
  • 4 green thai chillies
  • 1/4 cup of yogurt (whole fat is ideal, can substitute whatever is comfortable)

Steps

  1. Heat one pot on medium high
  2. Wash the meat with water and turmeric.
  3. Once the pot has warmed up, add the oil and the "whole" garam masala.
  4. Add onions and gently stir periodically until they become translucent (~30 min).
  5. Add salt as needed, to taste and to speed up frying.
  6. Add the ginger garlic paste, stir.
  7. Add the chillies.
  8. Add the measured turmeric once the onions are mushy and more transparent.
  9. Add the chili powder.
  10. Add the powdered garam masala.
  11. In the other pot, begin boiling water for rice. 4 parts of water for every part of rice.
  12. Add some salt to this.
  13. Add 1 tbsp. of turmeric and cover.
  14. Wash rice with warm water and let soak for ~fifteen minutes after the last wash. Do not drain the final wash. When the pot begins to boil, then drain and add the rice.
  15. Remove this pot from heat once the rice is parboiled.
  16. In the first pot, add the chicken pieces and stir.
  17. Mix in the yogurt.
  18. Squeeze the juice of half a lemon into the pot and stir.
  19. Add the cilantro, fenugreek, and mint leaves in the ratio described in the ingredients section.
  20. Chop carrots, 2 inches wide, and add to the pot.
  21. Wash frozen peas in a bowl, drain, and add to the pot.
  22. Once rice has parboiled, gently stir it and its water into the other pot. Try not to break the grains.
  23. Turn the heat to high and cover the pot.
  24. Once the pot begins to boil and steam heavily, place a flat pan (or the other lid) underneath the pot and reduce the heat to low.
  25. Pick a side of the pot and gently dig the spoon down the side and into the bottom layer. Fold this layer up and over towards the center.
  26. Do this three more times, across from the side you just chose, then a side between the first and second, then across from the third.
  27. Add foil across the top of the pot, tuck it over and under the lip, then cover with the lid.
  28. Let this sit for about half an hour. Use the firmness of the rice as a guide.
  29. Add salt to taste.
